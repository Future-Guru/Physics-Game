Pip’s Very Important Delivery

Meet Pip, the smallest courier at the Interplanetary Postal Service. Today Pip is carrying one slice of birthday cake to Grandma Quasar, who is turning 4.6 billion and lives at the far edge of the map.

The catch: the postal service blew its fuel budget on a very large hat. Pip gets a couple of tiny burns, tops.

The good news: gravity is free. Every star and planet will tug Pip along if you let it. Fall toward them, swing around them, and borrow a little of their speed. Just don’t crash. Cake is not a crash-test dummy.

You’re Pip’s navigator. Pull back to aim, let go, and let physics do the driving.
